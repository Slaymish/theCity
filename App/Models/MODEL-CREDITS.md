# Models

- `City/`: KayKit City Builder Bits 1.0 by Kay Lousberg (www.kaylousberg.com), CC0. Converted from glTF by `Assets/Pipeline/convert.py`. `City/tree.usdz` is the KayKit bush's canopy on a trunk, built by `Assets/Pipeline/tree.py`.
- `Nature/`: KayKit Medieval Hexagon Pack 1.0 by Kay Lousberg (www.kaylousberg.com), CC0. Rocks and trees converted from glTF by `Assets/Pipeline/convert.py`; `grass_tuft`, `flowers_A`, `flowers_B` and `pebbles` are built from the pack's atlas by `Assets/Pipeline/foliage.py`. The woodland `tree_*` models are from the Kenney Nature Kit 2.1 by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), CC0, repainted from the KayKit atlas by `Assets/Pipeline/nature.py`.
- `Furniture/`: KayKit Furniture Bits 1.0 by Kay Lousberg (www.kaylousberg.com), CC0. Converted from glTF by `Assets/Pipeline/convert.py`.
- `robot.usdz`, `monitor.usdz`, `keyboard.usdz`, `mug.usdz`: modelled for The City by `Assets/Pipeline/robot.py`.
