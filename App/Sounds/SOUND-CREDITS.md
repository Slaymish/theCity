# Sounds

From Kenney's Interface Sounds, Impact Sounds and UI Audio packs (www.kenney.nl), CC0. Converted from OGG to AAC with ffmpeg.

- `whoosh`: maximize_003 · `boop`: question_001 · `ping`: confirmation_002 · `ding`: bong_001 · `tick`: tick_001 (Interface Sounds)
- `stamp`: impactPunch_medium_000 · `deny`: impactSoft_heavy_000 · `drop`: impactPlate_light_000 · `bell`: impactBell_heavy_001 · `thunk`: impactWood_heavy_001 (Impact Sounds)
- `patter`: eight of the click_00x sounds (Interface Sounds) laid into a 2.4 s loop.
